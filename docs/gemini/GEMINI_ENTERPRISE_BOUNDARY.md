# Gemini Enterprise Boundary

## Role

Google Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform may be used as an enterprise AI agent substrate.

Allowed roles:

- summarization
- retrieval
- enterprise search
- workflow assistance
- agent orchestration
- policy explanation
- evidence package drafting
- control narrative assistance

## Not Allowed

Gemini Enterprise must not independently:

- create authoritative evidence
- approve compliance status
- mutate production systems
- issue credentials or tokens
- make enforcement decisions
- bypass SecureTheCloud governance
- claim certification
- override doctrine
- replace human or governed approval

## Design Principle

Gemini assists. SecureTheCloud governs.

# Module Authority Model

## Purpose

This document defines the authority level of initial SecureTheCloud Trust Intelligence Platform modules.

## Authority Levels

### Advisory

May assist with summaries, recommendations, drafting, and explanations.

### Evidence-Linked

May reference evidence and control mappings, but must preserve traceability.

### Governance-Controlled

May influence governed workflows only through approved review paths.

### Forbidden

Must not perform runtime enforcement, production mutation, credential issuance, or certification approval.

## Initial Modules

| Module | Authority Level | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Trust Intelligence Copilot | Advisory | Explains, summarizes, retrieves, and drafts. Does not approve or enforce. |
| Evidence Intelligence Registry | Evidence-Linked | Organizes evidence references. Does not fabricate evidence. |
| Compliance Operations Center | Governance-Controlled | Supports control workflows. Does not certify compliance. |
| Audit Workspace | Evidence-Linked | Prepares auditor-facing packages. Does not replace audit judgment. |
| Risk Intelligence | Advisory | Produces risk interpretation. Does not alter production risk state. |
| Agent Orchestration Layer | Advisory | Coordinates agent tasks. Does not grant agents authority. |
| Executive Trust Dashboard | Evidence-Linked | Displays governed summaries. Does not create new truth. |

## Non-Negotiable Boundary

No module may override doctrine, governance records, approved evidence, or human/governed approval.

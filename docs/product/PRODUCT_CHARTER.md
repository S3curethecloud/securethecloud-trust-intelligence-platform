# Product Charter

## Product Name

SecureTheCloud Trust Intelligence Platform

## Purpose

A governed AI-assisted platform for enterprise trust, compliance, risk, audit, and evidence operations.

## Enterprise Value

The platform helps organizations answer:

- Are we audit ready?
- What evidence supports each control?
- Which risks threaten compliance posture?
- What changed since the last audit package?
- Which controls need owner action?
- What can be safely summarized for executives, auditors, and customers?

## Primary Users

- CISOs
- compliance leaders
- security teams
- auditors
- GRC teams
- platform owners
- executive stakeholders

## Initial Capabilities

- evidence intelligence
- compliance control mapping
- audit package preparation
- governed trust copilot
- risk summaries
- executive reporting
- agent-assisted workflows

## Authority Position

This platform assists trust operations. It does not independently certify, enforce, mutate, or approve production trust state.

# Product Doctrine

## Product

SecureTheCloud Trust Intelligence Platform

## Doctrine Statement

SecureTheCloud Trust Intelligence Platform is a governed AI-assisted trust operations platform for enterprise compliance, audit, risk, and evidence intelligence.

The platform may use enterprise agent substrates, including Google Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform, to assist users with retrieval, summarization, workflow support, and governed explanation.

The platform must not delegate authority to any AI model, agent, external workflow, or third-party substrate.

## Authority Principle

SecureTheCloud governs.

Agents assist.

Evidence must be traceable.

Claims must be bounded.

Approvals must remain governed.

## Primary Trust Functions

- evidence intelligence
- compliance mapping
- audit package preparation
- risk interpretation
- control explanation
- executive trust reporting
- policy and governance assistance

## Forbidden Product Claims

The platform must not claim:

- SOC 2 certification
- ISO certification
- production operating effectiveness
- automated audit approval
- autonomous compliance certification
- independent enforcement authority
- customer environment truth without governed evidence

## Product Boundary

This repository defines the product foundation and governed trust intelligence layer.

Runtime integrations, customer data ingestion, production enforcement, credential use, and live Gemini Enterprise integration require a future governed phase.
